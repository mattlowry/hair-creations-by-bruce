How to Maintain Salon Color & Brazilian Blowouts in Virginia Humidity

From humid July afternoons along the Potomac to dry winter indoor heating, Northern Virginia’s climate puts hair color and smoothing treatments to the test. Follow these studio-proven habits to protect your investment.

Ask Bruce a Question: (571) 213-3667 Explore Salon Services

1. Why Sulfate-Free & Sodium Chloride-Free Products Are Non-Negotiable

Harsh lauryl sulfates and salts strip away both microscopic color pigments and the amino-acid protein shield of a Brazilian Blowout. Switching to a professional sulfate-free cleanser and rinsing with lukewarm (rather than steaming hot) water keeps the cuticle tightly sealed.

2. Pairing Color with Non-Peroxide Gloss Refreshes

If your blonde highlights turn brassy from hard mineral water or your brunette loses its mirror shine between full color appointments, schedule a quick Non-Peroxide Color Gloss or Redken/Goldwell toner refresh with Bruce to restore rich depth without chemical lift.
