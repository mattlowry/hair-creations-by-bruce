Frequently Asked Questions & First-Visit Guide

Everything you need to know before your appointment at Hair Creations by Bruce in McLean, Virginia.

Where is Hair Creations by Bruce located and where do I park?

We are located inside the McLean House Condo Building (Bruce @ Salon Dyanne) at 6800 Fleetwood Rd, Suite 106, McLean, VA 22101. Convenient surface visitor parking is available right at the building in downtown McLean.

What are Bruce’s hours and do I need an appointment?

Bruce operates by appointment only Monday through Saturday from 6:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Our early 6:00 AM opening is ideal for executives, commuters, and clients who want a quiet morning appointment before work.

Can I bring my own hair extensions or men’s hair system for Bruce to install?

Yes! "If you have your own hair, bring it and I will put it in." Bruce installs client-supplied 100% Remy extensions (BYOH installation starting at $250+), women’s wigs ($120+), and men’s hair replacement systems of all base types ($125–$165 with cut-in).

What professional hair color brands do you use?

We formulate with Wella Color, Goldwell Colors, L’Oréal Professionnel, and Redken—plus gentle Non-Peroxide and Ammonia-Free color options for sensitive scalps.

Do you travel to private homes, senior living facilities, or hospitals?

Yes, Bruce is available for in-home, assisted living, nursing facility, and hospital haircare visits throughout McLean and Northern Virginia, and also provides Men’s Personal Valet Services and Expert Sterling Silver Cleaning.

What forms of payment do you accept?

For your convenience, we accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover) as well as cash.
