Why Clients from Reston Choose Hair Creations by Bruce

Whether you are commuting east toward Tysons/DC in the early morning or visiting on a Saturday, Bruce offers Reston clients specialized extension maintenance ($95/hr micro-link tightening), BYOH installations ($250+), and confidential men’s hair replacement.

Popular Salon & Mobile Services for Reston Residents

  • 100% Remy Hair Extensions & BYOH Installation: Micro-Links, Wefts, and Micro-Fusion bonds in Straight, Curly, Deep Curl, and Italian Wave.
  • Custom Wella, Goldwell, L’Oréal & Redken Color: Single-process color ($110+), Cut & Color Finish ($165+), and Non-Peroxide gentle color ($125+).
  • Balayage & Dimensional Highlights: Partial highlights ($145+) and full-head hand-painted balayage or foils ($225+).
  • Confidential Men’s Hair Systems & Toupees: Application with cut-in ($125–$165) and $95 maintenance visits—bring your own unit or order custom.
  • Men’s Precision Haircuts ($45) & Women’s Cut + Blowout ($85): Open at 6:00 AM Mon–Sat for effortless pre-commute scheduling.
  • In-Home & Assisted Living Visits in Reston: Mobile haircare, Men’s Personal Valet Services, and Expert Sterling Silver Cleaning.
