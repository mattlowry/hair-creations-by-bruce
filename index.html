The Independent Studio Difference

One-on-One Craftsmanship Without the Big-Salon Markup

As an independent master stylist at Salon Dyanne inside the McLean House Condo Building, Bruce offers something increasingly rare in Northern Virginia: dedicated one-on-one appointments where the master stylist personally handles every step of your haircut, color formulation, extension placement, or hair replacement system.

From Brazilian Blowouts, Keratin Smoothing Treatments, and Non-Peroxide Gentle Colors to Copper & Acrylic Micro-Link Extensions, Women’s Wig Sew-Ins, and Men’s Toupee Maintenance, Bruce serves discerning clients across McLean, Tysons Corner, Arlington, Vienna, Great Falls, Falls Church, Fairfax, Reston, Oakton, Washington DC, and Montgomery County.

  • LGBTQ+ Friendly & Inclusive Sanctuary: A welcoming, private, judgment-free studio environment for every guest.
  • Four World-Class Color Houses: Custom-formulated with Wella Color, Goldwell Colors, L’Oréal Professionnel, and Redken.
  • Bring Your Own Hair (BYOH) Friendly: Have your own Remy hair extensions, wefts, wig, or men’s hairpiece? Bring it in and Bruce will professionally install, blend, and finish it.
  • Available for Home & Senior Facilities: Compassionate mobile visits for homebound clients and senior communities—plus Men’s Personal Valet Services & Expert Sterling Silver Cleaning.
